# Password Reset Policy

- Passwords expire every 90 days.
- To reset your password, go to the self-service portal at password.company.com.
- You need your work email and access to your MFA app.
- New passwords must have at least 12 characters, with one number and one symbol.
- You cannot reuse your last 5 passwords.
- After 5 failed login attempts, your account is locked. The helpdesk can unlock it after verifying your identity.
