1.⁠ ⁠Download the company VPN client from the Software Portal.
2.⁠ ⁠Install it and restart your computer.
3.⁠ ⁠Open the VPN client and enter the server address: vpn.company.com
4.⁠ ⁠Sign in with your work email and password, then approve the MFA prompt on your phone.
5.⁠ ⁠Wait for the status to show "Connected".

Troubleshooting:
•⁠  ⁠If the VPN will not connect, check your internet connection first.
•⁠  ⁠If you see "authentication failed", your account may be locked. Contact the helpdesk.
•⁠  ⁠If the problem continues after restarting the client, escalate to the network team.
